## Make sure it is a cooler box

A cooler box, for this note, is one insulated box made to hold cold food or drink for a while, with a lid and a body you can carry or wheel. It may be a hard plastic box with a hinged lid. It may be a soft bag with a zip. It may have a telescopic handle and two wheels. It may sit on a shelf with the lid shut. Write the shape you can see. “Hard box, hinged lid, no wheels.” “Soft bag, zip shut, shoulder strap.” “Wheeled box, handle down, drain plug at the bottom.”

The lid is not a second box. If the lid is on the box, they are one line, and you say so. If the lid has come off and is beside the box, they are still one line when that lid belongs to that box. Write “lid with the box” or “lid off, beside the box”. Do not book the lid as a tray. Do not book the box as a suitcase. A set of suitcases is the [suitcase note](/blog/suitcases-after-a-trip-dubai). Do not put the cooler inside a suitcase and call the result luggage.

A fridge is not a cooler box. A fridge has a door, a plug and a cabinet that stands on a floor as an appliance. That machine stays on its own guide. Do not unplug a fridge and call it a cooler. Do not follow fridge steps, if you have them, on a box that was never plugged in.

A plumbed drinking-water cabinet is not this box. A cabinet with a tube into the wall, or a bottle that lifts off the top of a cooler cabinet, is the [plumbed-dispenser note](/blog/plumbed-water-dispenser-removal-dubai). Do not isolate a pipe because a picnic box was in the same kitchen. Do not call this box a bottle cooler. If a bottle cooler cabinet is also leaving, it is its own line.

A dehumidifier is not a cooler box. A unit with a tank in a closed room is the [dehumidifier note](/blog/dehumidifier-removal-dubai). Do not tip a tank and call it a drain plug. There is no tank on this page.

A plastic pet carrier is not a cooler box. A hard box with a grille door, made for an animal to travel in, is the [pet-carrier note](/blog/pet-carrier-removal-dubai). Do not empty a water bowl and call the carrier a cooler. Do not follow the grille steps on a box that has a lid and no door. A live animal is not a line on this page.

A stack of plastic crates left after an unpack is not this box. Those crates are the [plastic-crate note](/blog/plastic-crates-after-unpacking-dubai). A cooler does not nest with them. Do not put the cooler inside a crate and call the result crates.

A laundry basket is not a cooler box. A stack of baskets is the [laundry-basket note](/blog/laundry-basket-stack-dubai). Do not empty wet clothes into a cooler and call the cooler a basket.

A sewing machine in its case is not this box. That machine is the [sewing-machine note](/blog/sewing-machine-removal-dubai). Do not put the machine inside the cooler to save a line.

A mannequin, a filing cabinet, a bookshelf and a partition are not given a URL here. If one of those is in the store, name it as what it is and keep it out of the photograph of the box.

If you are not sure, write the shape. “White box, thick walls, lid with a catch, cold inside” is enough. “Black bag, silver lining, zip, strap” is enough. A wrong name is worse than a plain description.

## Empty the box before you call the lid empty

Start with the lid, because that is where food and meltwater hide.

Open the lid only if it already opens by the catch, the clip or the zip it has. If the catch will not move, leave the lid shut and write “lid will not open”. Do not pry it with a knife, and do not cut the seal to see inside. A box you have not opened is not an empty box. The honest line is the shut lid.

If it opens, look in the body, not only at the lid. Bottles come out. Food comes out. Ice packs come out. A loose cube of ice comes out. A drip tray, if it already lifts, comes out. A cloth, a fork and a spare cap come out. This page does not book the food, and it does not tell you which pile a bottle belongs in. If you are keeping them, put them where you will still find them, off the photograph. If you have not decided, put them in one open pile that is not the leaving box, and write “contents, not decided”.

Do not stuff the bottles back in to make the photo look tidy. Do not bag the food and put the bag back in the box and call the box empty. The bag is a second object if it is leaving on its own. If the small parts are leaving with the box, the bag stays with the box and you write “parts bagged with the box”. You still have to be able to say the bag is not a second box.

Look under a false floor, if the box has one and it already lifts by hand. A drain plug, a key and a spare strap hide there. A key stays with you. Anything you still need does not ride inside a box that is leaving.

Then the water. Tip what you can tip into a sink or a drain you already use, if the box is light enough to lift. Write “water out”. If the box is too heavy to tip, find the drain plug. If the plug already unscrews or pulls by hand, open it over a tray or a floor you can mop, and write “drain plug open, water out”. Shut the plug again when the water has gone, if it still shuts, and write “drain plug shut”. If the plug will not move, leave it and write “drain plug stuck, water still in” or “drain plug stuck, no water seen”. Do not drill a hole in the box to let the water out. Do not cut the plug off.

An ice pack that is still frozen is not the box. Take it out. If you are keeping it, put it in the freezer you are keeping, off the photograph. If you have decided it is going, it is its own small line or it is bagged with the box, and you say which. Do not leave a frozen pack in the box and call the box empty. A pack that has split and leaked gets the words “pack split”. Do not taste it to see what it is. Do not pour an unknown gel into the sink and call the box empty. Write “gel, not identified” and keep that pack out of the box, in a bag you can point at.

Do not hose the box as a wash. Water you add has to go somewhere, and a wet box is heavier. If the lining smells, say so in plain words: “empty, smells, not washed”. Do not spray a chemical into the box and then shut the lid. This page does not say what to spray, and it does not name a place to take a bottle of cleaner. If a bottle of cleaner is in the box, it comes out. If you do not know what the bottle is, write “bottle, not identified” and do not pour it out.

A label with a name, an account or an address comes off if it already peels. If it does not peel, leave it and write “label still on”. Do not scrape a shelf to remove a sticker that is on the shelf rather than on the box.

If you cannot see the bottom because the lid will not open, do not cut a window in the lid. Write “inside not seen” and what you believe is still in the box. That box stays out of any sentence that says it is empty.

## A lid that will not shut, and a handle that will not fold

What makes one box a problem is the lid, the water, and a part that sticks out.

Try the lid or the zip the way it was built to close, after the box is empty or after you have written why it is not. If it shuts, shut it and write “lid shuts”. If it stops on a bottle, the bottle is not out. Go back one step. If the seal is torn and the lid will not sit, write “lid will not shut” and leave it as it sits. Do not remove the lid by breaking the hinge. Do not stand on the lid to flatten it. Do not tape the lid shut as a repair. Tape is not a hinge. If the box already has a strap, you may use that strap and write “strapped with its own strap”. Do not invent a closure.

A soft bag that already zips can be zipped. If the zip stops on a bottle or a strap, write “zip will not close” and do not cut the bag. A bag is not a reason to hide the shape. You will measure the box or the closed bag, and you will say which one you measured.

The telescopic handle is not a second box. If it already slides down by the button it has, slide it down and write “handle down”. If it will not slide, leave it up and write “handle stuck up”. Do not saw the handle off to make a shorter shape. Do not bend it flat. The handle is part of the shape you measure if it stays up.

Wheels that are still on stay on. If a wheel has already come off in your hands, bag it with the box and write “wheel off, bagged with box”. Do not glue it back on as a preparation step. Do not take the axle out to make the box narrower.

A shoulder strap that already unclips can come off for the photo, then go back on if that is how the box will travel. Write “strap on” or “strap off, with the box”. Do not cut the strap to make a shorter shape.

A split lining, a cracked corner and a lid that hangs on one hinge are the shape you have. Write what you can see. “Corner cracked.” “Lining split.” “One hinge left.” Do not pick pieces out to make it look whole. A piece that has already come off goes in a bag with the box. Write “broken piece, bagged with box”. Do not tape the crack as a repair.

If you have already forced the lid and the hinge is split, stop. Write “hinge split” and do not press it back together. The photo has to show the box you have.

Count after you have looked, not before. “One cooler box” is a count when you can point at one. “The camping things” is not a count. A second box in the same room is a second line, with its own lid and its own drain check. Do not add them in your head and write one number you did not check. If you move them together, count again after you move them.

## Where the box is standing

The box is awkward because of where it was left, as well as because of the lid.

On a high shelf in the store, do not drag it down by the strap. Lift what you can hold, or leave it and write “on the high shelf, not brought down”. A box that falls and splits is a different shape from the one you were going to measure. If water is still in it, it is heavier than it looks. Empty it before you lift it, if you can reach the lid where it sits. If you cannot reach the lid, write “on the shelf, not opened” and do not climb a stack of cartons to get there.

Wedged between a shelf and a wall, do not kick it out. If it slides by hand, slide it and write “slid out”. If the shelf has to move first and the shelf is staying, do not drag the shelf off its feet to reach a box. Write “behind the shelf, not pulled out” and photograph it where it is.

Under a table or a spare mattress, do not drag it out by the handle. If it slides by hand, slide it and write “slid out”. If a drawer or a mattress blocks it and that piece is staying, do not pull the mattress off the frame. Write “under the mattress, not pulled out” and photograph it where it is.

In a garage, the garage is not the box. The [garage guide](/blog/garage-clearance-dubai) keeps the tools, the paint and the mixed inventory. One cooler box is this note. Do not call the box a tool case. Do not hide it inside a carton of paint and call the result boxes. Paint, fuel and a bottle you cannot name stay out of the cooler and out of this note. This page does not say where a tin of paint goes.

In a shared corridor or a bin room, do not leave the box there the night before to “get it out of the flat”. The place you photograph is the place it leaves from. A box you have not opened does not belong on a landing. Water that leaks overnight is a different problem from the box you started with.

Beside a pile of cartons, the cartons stay on the [guide to sorting packing material](/blog/packing-material-disposal-dubai) if that is the pile. Do not call the box a carton. Do not hide the box inside a carton and call the result boxes. A box used as a weight on a stack of magazines is still a cooler. The magazines are not this page.

On a balcony, the balcony is not the box. A mixed balcony stays on the [balcony guide](/blog/balcony-clearance-dubai) if that is the job. One cooler box is this note. A drying rack stays with the [drying-rack note](/blog/drying-rack-removal-dubai). Do not strap the box to the rack and call them one object.

## Measure the box as it will travel

Measure after you have emptied the box, or after you have written why you could not, and not with a guess.

If the box will travel with the lid shut, measure the box: height, width and depth, including a handle that sticks up and a wheel that sticks out. Say “measured shut”. If the lid will not shut, measure the shape you actually have, and write “lid open in the measure”. If the handle is stuck up, include it. A strap left on can change the depth. Say whether the strap is on.

Then look at the opening it has to pass. The useful figures are the clear height and clear width of the narrowest door, with the door as open as it goes. A box on a high shelf still has to pass that door if it is coming out. A shelf, a carton or a bicycle can change the gap. Move what you are keeping, then measure if the gap changed.

If you have no tape, write “not measured”. Do not invent the numbers. This note does not keep a standard size for a cooler box, a lid or a doorway.

Write one sentence you measured. “Lid shut, handle taller than the gap beside the shelf” or “lid open, not yet compared with the door” or “not measured”. Do not write “it will fit” if you have not compared the figures. Do not write “it will not fit” from memory of a different door.

Weight is not something to guess. A box you cannot lift because it is still full of water is not a weight. Empty it first, or write why you could not. If the empty box is awkward because of the handle, write “handle up, awkward to carry”. Do not write a number you did not weigh. Do not write a crew size. This note does not keep one.

The wider method for one object and the narrowest point on the way out stays with the [bulky-item note](/blog/bulky-item-pickup-dubai) if the corridor and the lift are the question. Stay on this page for the lid, the water and the count. Do not send two descriptions that disagree about whether the box was empty.

## One line in the booking

Say, in one message:

- one cooler box, hard or soft or wheeled, and where it stands
- inside empty, or lid will not open, and what you believe is inside
- lid shuts, lid will not shut, or no lid
- water out, or drain plug stuck and whether water is still in
- handle down, handle stuck up, or no handle
- the height and the widths you measured, or “not measured”
- anything else leaving from the same room, as its own line, including a pet carrier, a suitcase or a crate if that object is going

Do not write “the store” as the only words if the room also holds a pet carrier, a suitcase and a pile of cartons you have not decided about. Do not write “empty” if you have not opened the lid. Do not ask for a handle to be cut because the figure is taller than the gap. The figure is the report. A cut is not a step on this page.

If you have not looked in the box, you are early. Open it if the catch already moves. If the catch will not move, the shut lid is the honest line. Send that.

On the morning, put the box where the photos put it. Check a bottle has not gone back in overnight. Check a drain you opened is shut again if you said it was shut. A cube of ice left inside is not an empty box. Take it out before you treat the old note as current.

Point at the body and the lid as the box. Point at a fridge, a carrier or a crate only if that object is its own line. The useful record is the sentence from the first check: one cooler box, emptied or the lid named, water out or the stuck plug written, lid shut or the reason written, kept as its own line, written in plain words.
