## Make sure it is a garden hose reel

A garden hose reel, for this note, is one reel made to hold a garden hose, standing by a tap or sitting free on the ground, with a hose you can see. It may be a cart with two wheels and a crank. It may be a wall bracket the reel already lifts off. It may be a freestanding frame with the hose wound on. It may be a loose coil with no reel at all, if that coil is the thing you meant. Write the shape you can see. “Cart, two wheels, hose wound, connector on the tap.” “Bracket on the wall, reel still on it.” “Loose coil, no reel, both ends on the ground.”

The hose is not a second reel when it belongs to that reel. If the hose is wound on, they are one line, and you say so. If the hose is off the reel and lying beside it, they are still one line when that hose belongs to that reel. Write “hose on the reel” or “hose off, beside the reel”. Do not book the hose as a rope. Do not book the reel as a bicycle. A bicycle is the [bicycle note](/blog/bicycle-removal-dubai). Do not coil the hose around a bike and call the result a bike.

A fire hose in a cabinet, or a reel fixed in a parking bay that crosses a path, is not this garden reel. That object stays where the building put it. The [parking-bay locker note](/blog/parking-bay-locker-clearance-dubai) is the locker and the path. Do not unwind a fire hose. Do not call a red cabinet a garden reel. If you are not sure which one you have, write the place. “Beside the villa tap, green hose” is this note. “In a red cabinet by the lift” is not.

A patio set is not a hose reel. Chairs, a table, a lounger and a parasol are the garden-furniture guide. Do not strap the reel to a chair and call them one set. If the set is also leaving, it is its own line.

Cuttings, fronds, soil and pots are not this reel. A whole garden of those materials is the villa-garden guide. Soil and pots, when they are the dense part, stay on the [soil and pots guide](/blog/soil-sand-pots-disposal-dubai). Do not stuff the hose into a bag of leaves and call the result garden waste. Do not hide the reel under a pile of fronds.

A trampoline is not a hose reel. That structure is the [trampoline note](/blog/trampoline-removal-dubai). Do not wind the hose around a leg and call the reel part of the frame.

A barbecue, a smoker and a patio heater are not this reel. They are the [grill note](/blog/bbq-grill-disposal-dubai). A gas cylinder is not a hose. Do not coil a hose around a cylinder. Do not detach a cylinder because a reel was beside the grill. This page does not collect a cylinder, and it does not say where a cylinder goes.

A water tank is not a hose reel. A hot tub is not a hose reel. An above-ground pool frame is not a hose reel. None of those is given a URL here. If one of them is in the garden, write the name you can see and leave it out of these steps. Do not drain a tank by calling it a hose. Do not saw a pool frame because a reel was leaning on it.

A ceiling fan is not this page. Do not book a fan by calling it a reel.

A pergola, a shed, a deck and a fence are not this reel. They are not given a URL on this page. If a post is in the way of the hose, write “post in the way” and do not cut the post.

A mannequin, a filing cabinet and a partition are not given a URL here. If one of those has been left in the garden, name it as what it is and keep it out of the photograph of the reel.

If you are not sure, write the shape. “Plastic cart, crank, green hose, brass end on a wall tap” is enough. “Metal bracket, hose hanging, tap still dripping” is enough. A wrong name is worse than a plain description.

## Drain the hose before you call the reel empty

Start with the water, because a hose that looks empty can still be heavy.

Find both ends. One end may be on the tap. One end may be on the reel, or lying in a bed. Follow the hose with your eyes before you pull. If you cannot see one end, write “one end not seen” and do not cut the hose to find it.

Turn the tap off if the handle already turns by hand. Write “tap off”. If the handle will not move, leave it and write “tap will not turn”. Do not hit the tap with a tool. Do not take the tap off the wall. The tap stays with the villa.

Unscrew the connector from the tap only if it already turns by hand. If it turns, take it off and write “off the tap”. If it will not turn, leave it and write “connector stuck on the tap”. Do not cut the hose at the tap. Do not use a knife on the fitting. A cut hose is a different object from the reel you started with, and a cut at the tap can leave a fitting you cannot name.

Then the water in the hose. Lift the free end so it is lower than the reel, if you can do that without pulling the tap. Let the water run out onto ground that already takes water, or into a bucket you can empty. Write “hose drained”. If water keeps coming and the tap is off, write “still dripping” and do not leave the end indoors. If you cannot drain it because one end is not seen, write “not drained”.

A nozzle, a spray gun and a spare connector come off if they already unscrew or unclip by hand. Bag them with the reel if they are leaving, or keep them if you are keeping them. Write which. If a nozzle will not move, leave it and write “nozzle stuck”. Do not saw it off. The nozzle is part of the shape you measure if it stays on.

Wind the hose back onto the reel only if the crank already turns, and only after the water is out or you have written why it is not. If it winds, wind it and write “wound”. If the crank sticks, leave the hose as it lies and write “crank stuck, hose not wound”. Do not cut the hose into lengths to make it look wound. Do not stand on the coil to flatten it.

A loose coil with no reel is still this note if that coil is the object. Drain it the same way. Write “no reel, coil only”. Do not invent a reel you do not have. Do not tape the coil into a circle and call it a reel.

Do not hose the reel down as a wash. Water you add has to go somewhere. If the cart is muddy, say so in plain words: “drained, muddy, not washed”. Do not spray a chemical on the hose and then wind it. This page does not say what to spray.

A label with a name or an address comes off if it already peels. If it does not peel, leave it and write “label still on”.

## A reel that will not come off the tap

What makes one reel a problem is the fitting, the bracket, and a hose that will not wind.

If the connector is off the tap, the reel is free of the wall at that end. Say so. If the connector is stuck, the reel is not free. Do not book it as free. The honest line is the stuck fitting.

A wall bracket is a fixing. If the reel already lifts off the bracket by the movement it was built with, lift it off and write “off the bracket”. Leave the bracket on the wall. The bracket is not the reel. If the reel will not lift off, leave it and write “still on the bracket”. Do not unscrew the bracket from the wall. Do not pull the plugs out of the render. This page does not take a fixing out of a wall.

A cart that stands on its own wheels is free if the hose is off the tap. Write “cart, free”. If a chain or a lock holds the cart to a post, and the lock is yours and already opens, open it and write “lock off”. If the lock is not yours, or you would have to cut it, stop. Write “locked to the post” and leave it. This page does not cut a lock.

A guide hose that runs along a wall and disappears into a bed is not a reel you can wind. Write “hose fixed along the wall” and do not dig it out. Do not cut it at the soil line and call the visible part a reel. The part you can lift, if any, is the only part this note can describe. Write “lifted the free length” or “none of it lifts”.

A split hose gets the words “hose split”. Do not pick the split open to make it drain faster. If water is still coming from the split, point the split at the ground and write “split, still leaking”. A piece that has already come off the cart goes in a bag with the reel, or beside it if it will not fit. Write “broken piece, with the reel”. Do not tape the split as a repair.

A crank handle that has already come off in your hands goes with the reel. Write “crank off, with the reel”. Do not glue it back on. If the crank is still on and stuck, leave it.

If you have already forced the bracket and the wall is marked, stop. Write “wall marked, stopped” and do not fill the hole. The photo has to show the reel you have, and the wall as it is.

Count after you have looked, not before. “One hose reel” is a count when you can point at one. “The garden hoses” is not a count. A second reel by a second tap is a second line, with its own drain and its own fitting. Do not add them in your head and write one number you did not check. If you move them together, count again after you move them.

## Where the reel is standing

The reel is awkward because of where it was left, as well as because of the hose.

By the villa tap, the tap stays. Move the reel only after the connector is off or you have written that it is stuck. Do not drag a cart while the hose is still on the tap. A short hose can pull the tap. Write “dragged while connected” only if that has already happened, and stop.

On a side path, a gate can close on the hose. If the hose is across the path, lift the free end and set it with the reel, if it already lifts. If a car or a bin is on the hose, do not pull. Write “under the car” or “under the bin” and photograph it there.

In a bed or under a shrub, do not yank the hose out by the reel. If it slides by hand, slide it and write “slid out”. If a root or a stake holds it, write “held in the bed” and do not dig. This page does not dig.

In a store or a garage, the room is not the reel. A packed store stays on the [storage-room guide](/blog/storage-room-clearance-dubai). A mixed garage stays on the [garage guide](/blog/garage-clearance-dubai). One reel you brought in from the tap is this note. Do not call it a tool. Do not hide it behind a tin of paint. Paint and a bottle you cannot name stay out of this note. This page does not say where a tin goes.

Beside a pile of chairs, the chairs stay on the garden-furniture guide. Do not put the reel on a lounger and call it furniture.

Beside a pile of fronds, the fronds stay on the villa-garden guide, or on the [palm-frond guide](/blog/palm-frond-disposal-dubai) if that is the pile. Do not call the reel a frond.

In a shared passage, do not leave the reel there the night before to “get it out of the garden”. The place you photograph is the place it leaves from. A hose you have not drained does not belong on a neighbour’s path.

## Measure the reel as it will travel

Measure after you have drained the hose, or after you have written why you could not, and not with a guess.

If the hose is wound and the reel is off the tap, measure the reel: height, width and depth, including a handle, a wheel and a nozzle that sticks out. Say “measured wound”. If the hose is not wound, measure the reel on its own and write the hose as a second figure: the length only if you can lay it out and measure it, or “hose not measured” if you cannot. Do not guess a length from memory of a different hose. Say “hose loose in the measure” if the loose hose changes the shape you will carry.

Then look at the opening it has to pass. The useful figures are the clear height and clear width of the narrowest gate or door, with that gate as open as it goes. A reel by the back tap still has to pass that gate if it is coming out. A chair, a pot or a bin can change the gap. Move what you are keeping, then measure if the gap changed.

If you have no tape, write “not measured”. Do not invent the numbers. This note does not keep a standard size for a reel, a hose or a gate.

Write one sentence you measured. “Wound, taller than the side gate” or “hose loose, not yet compared with the gate” or “not measured”. Do not write “it will fit” if you have not compared the figures. Do not write “it will not fit” from memory of a different gate.

Weight is not something to guess. A hose you cannot lift because it is still full of water is not a weight. Drain it first, or write why you could not. If the empty cart is awkward because of the wheels, write “awkward to roll”. Do not write a number you did not weigh. Do not write a crew size. This note does not keep one.

The wider method for one object and the narrowest point on the way out stays with the [bulky-item note](/blog/bulky-item-pickup-dubai) if the path and the gate are the question. Stay on this page for the drain, the tap and the count. Do not send two descriptions that disagree about whether the hose was empty.
